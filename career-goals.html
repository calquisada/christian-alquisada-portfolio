My Career Goals

As an aspiring software engineer with a diverse background in healthcare IT and full-stack development, my long-term vision is to shape innovative technology that makes a real difference in people’s lives.

One of my top goals is to join a leading tech company—such as Google, Microsoft, or Amazon—where I can contribute to large-scale projects and collaborate with world-class engineers. Working at a major company will allow me to sharpen my problem-solving skills, learn from some of the best minds in the industry, and work on products that impact millions of users.

In the future, I also plan to launch my own startup. I’m passionate about building tools and platforms that solve real-world problems, especially in areas like education, healthcare, or accessibility. I believe the entrepreneurial path will challenge me to grow as both a technologist and a leader.
